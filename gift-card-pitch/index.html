Quick guide

Objection handling

  • Acknowledge what they said before anything else.
  • Ask one question to find the real reason (price, timing, use).
  • Adjust: step down a card level, or tie it to what they already spend.
  • Let it go after one redirect. Leave them happy and note it for next visit.
Close it today

Make today the easy yes

Credit goes to whoever rings it up, so aim to finish the sale while you're with them. Keep it honest: real reasons, no fake deadlines.

Use today's checkout

“Since you're already checking out, I can put it toward today. You get the Day Pass right now instead of waiting.”

It's quick now

“I've already got your numbers pulled up. It takes about a minute to set up while you're here.”

Be honest about your goal

“Honestly, I'm working toward a holiday sales goal, and I'd love to be the one to set this up for you since I already worked out what fits your visits.”

Ask which, not if

“Would the $250 or the $450 work better for you? Either way, I'll put it toward today.”

Fallback · Smallest yes

If they hesitate on bigger cards, offer the $250 on today's total. It's the least they'll spend for a free Day Pass, and it still counts as your sale.

Common objections

What they say

Tap one for what to say back.

“I need to think about it.”Usually means they're unsure it's worth it, not that they said no.
Redirect

Totally fair. Is it the amount, or not sure you'll use it? Since it goes toward today's total, you'd get the Day Pass right now. And honestly, I'm working on a holiday goal, so I'd love to help you get it set up while you're here.

If they still say no

Offer the smallest yes: “Even the $250 toward today gets you the Day Pass, and you're spending most of that already.” If it's still no, thank them warmly and let it go.

“I need to check with my partner.”Shared money. Make it easy to say yes later.
Redirect

Of course. Want me to jot down the numbers so you can show them? It's $[pay] for $[value] plus [passes].

If they still say no

Couples' visits are a great way to use it, so the $450 or $900 card usually makes the conversation easy.

“I'll do it next time.”Credit only goes to whoever rings it up, so try once to close it today.
Redirect

I get it! The nice thing about doing it now is it goes toward today's visit, so you get the Day Pass today instead of next time. Plus I'm working toward a holiday sales goal, and I'd really love to be the one to help you with it.

If they still say no

Try the smallest yes ($250 toward today). If it's still no, smile, thank them, and leave it there. A guest who leaves happy is more likely to say yes next time.

“That's more than I want to spend right now.”Price shock. Step down, don't argue.
Redirect

Makes sense. The $250 card still gets you the free Day Pass, and it goes toward what you're paying today anyway.

If they still say no

If even that's too much today, no worries. Your total stays the same, and you can grab it anytime before the promo ends.

“I'm a member, I don't need a gift card.”They see it as a gift for someone else, not a way to pay.
Redirect

It's actually a great fit for members. You put it toward your monthly membership, gratuity and upgrades, and you get the bonus value and Day Passes on top. Same money, more value.

If they still say no

If you'd rather keep paying the usual way, that's fine too. Just know the bonus is only during the holidays.

“I'm in a hurry.”They're leaving. Keep it to one sentence.
Redirect

Super quick: you're at $[total] today. If I put $250 on a gift card, you get a free $75 Day Pass. Want me to do that?

If they still say no

No worries at all. I'll note it on your account and we can set it up next visit.

“I already finished my holiday shopping.”They think it's only a gift.
Redirect

Nice! A lot of people are actually buying these for themselves. It covers your own visits and you keep the Day Pass.

If they still say no

If they have someone left on their list: the $450 card works as a $500 gift, and you keep the Day Pass.

“What if I don't use it all?”Fear of waste.
Redirect

Because it covers things you already pay for, like gratuity, upgrades and your membership, most guests use it up within a few visits.

If they still say no

Check our current gift card terms before talking about expiration, so you give accurate info.

“Can I just buy the Day Pass?”They like the perk.
Redirect

The Day Pass is free with the gift card, so you'd get it plus the full card value. It's better than buying it alone.

If they still say no

If they truly only want the pass, ring it up normally and mention the card again next visit.
